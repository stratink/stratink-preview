Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be set on publication.

This Privacy Policy explains how Stratink Technologies Inc. ("Stratink", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information through the STRATINK™ website and app. It is drafted under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Who we are

Stratink Technologies Inc. is a British Columbia company. Our Privacy Officer can be reached at privacy@stratink.ca.

2. Information we collect

  • Website form: name, role, building name and city, number of units and email, when you apply to be a Founding Building.
  • Contact emails: information you include when you email us.
  • In the app: account and unit information, owner or tenant role, contact details, notification preferences, maintenance requests and attached photos, notice acknowledgements, and documents uploaded by councils or managers.
  • Technical data: basic log and device information needed to operate and secure the service.

2A. Building data we process for strata corporations

When a strata corporation, its council or its property manager uses STRATINK™, information in that building's account (such as owner and tenant contact details, unit information, maintenance requests, notices and strata records) is collected and controlled by the strata corporation. Stratink processes it as a service provider on the strata corporation's behalf and under its instructions. The strata corporation decides what information is uploaded and who can see it, and is responsible for having the authority to share it. Requests to access or correct strata records should go to your strata council or property manager; we will help them respond. When a building stops using STRATINK™, we return or delete its data as set out in our agreement with the strata corporation.

3. Why we collect it

We use personal information only to: assess pilot applications and contact applicants; provide, support and secure STRATINK™ for your building; respond to enquiries; and improve the service. We send news, product updates and offers by email only if you tick the optional marketing box (express consent under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation); every such email identifies us and includes an unsubscribe link, and we act on unsubscribe requests within 10 business days. We will not use it for other purposes without your consent, unless PIPA permits or requires it.

4. Consent and withdrawal

We collect personal information with your consent, for example by your checking the consent box on our form, or as permitted by PIPA. For building data in the app, we rely on the consent and authority obtained by the strata corporation (see section 2A). You may withdraw consent on reasonable notice by contacting our Privacy Officer, subject to legal or contractual limits. Withdrawing consent may affect our ability to provide the service.

5. Sharing and service providers

In the app, information is visible to others in your building according to their role (for example, your council or property manager can see maintenance requests you submit). We use service providers such as hosting, email and form providers to run STRATINK™, under confidentiality obligations. Our service providers, and the countries where they store or process personal information, will be listed here before launch. Information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts, law enforcement or national security authorities. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

  • Pilot applications: kept for 12 months after the pilot selection decision, then deleted, unless your building joins. Information used to make a decision about you is kept for at least one year after that decision, as PIPA requires.
  • App account data: kept while the building's account is active, and deleted or returned within 90 days after the strata corporation's account ends, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Backups and other records: retained only as long as needed for the purposes described, then securely deleted. Specific periods will be set before launch.

7. Safeguards

We make reasonable security arrangements to protect personal information against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal or similar risks.

8. Access and correction

You may ask to see or correct your personal information by contacting our Privacy Officer. We will respond within 30 business days, as PIPA requires, unless an extension is permitted.

9. Privacy incidents

If a privacy breach occurs, we will contain it, assess the risk, and notify affected individuals and the strata corporation without unreasonable delay where there is a real risk of significant harm. We will report to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia and/or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada where required or appropriate. We keep a record of every breach.

10. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

11. Children

STRATINK™ is not directed at children.

12. Questions and complaints

Please contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@stratink.ca first. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (www.oipc.bc.ca).

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version on this page with its effective date.
