Features

Everything your building needs, in one place

Council dashboard

Track maintenance requests, issue notices and keep decisions and documents organised in one place.

Resident app

Residents get building news, documents and updates on their phone, and can report issues in a few taps.

Notices and announcements

Reach every resident at once, so no one misses an important update.

Maintenance requests
