## For strata councils
- Run the building from one council dashboard: requests, notices, documents and decisions in one place.
- Spend less time on email chains and paper notices, and more time on decisions.
- Keep records organised and easy to find when owners ask.
- Communicate clearly with every resident at once.

## For residents
- Get building news, notices and documents on your phone.
- Report an issue in seconds and see when it's handled.
- Book trusted local services through the STRATINK™ marketplace.
- Feel more connected to your building and neighbours.

## For property managers
Work alongside your council with shared, organised communication instead of scattered threads.
