The problem

What STRATINK™ solves

A single branded platform for everyday resident needs and council administration.

Scattered communication

Notices, emails, paper postings and calls create confusion for residents.

Manual tracking

Maintenance requests, parking and bookings can be difficult to follow.

Hard-to-find records

Bylaws, forms and AGM documents need a central resident-friendly hub.

Admin workload

Council and management need one dashboard for action and visibility.

STRATINK™ turns repeated building tasks into a clean digital workflow.

Features

Everything your building needs, in one place

The resident app and council dashboard cover the everyday work of running a strata building.

Council dashboard

Track maintenance requests, issue notices and keep decisions and documents organised in one place.

Maintenance requests

Residents submit requests with details and photos, and the council or manager can follow each one through to resolution.

Notice board

One place for urgent alerts, scheduled maintenance and governance reminders. Publish building-wide or targeted announcements from the dashboard.

Documents & bylaws

Bylaws, AGM minutes, move-in rules, forms and building records in one place, so residents can find official documents without searching email.

My Requests

Residents follow progress on their requests (submitted, in progress, resolved and closed) without calling or emailing for updates.

Visitor parking

Residents register guest licence plates, vehicle details and active time windows.

Amenity bookings

Residents reserve shared spaces such as party rooms, lounges, elevators or meeting rooms.

Emergency contacts

Property manager, caretaker, after-hours number and critical building contacts, one tap away.

Resident profiles

Unit information, owner or tenant role, contact details and notification preferences.

How maintenance works

From report to resolved, without the phone tag

Residents submit a request, then follow progress without calling or emailing for updates.

  1. 1

    Create a ticket

    Category, priority, description and an optional photo become a trackable item.

  2. 2

    Track it in My Requests

    Residents can see submitted, in progress, resolved and closed items.

  3. 3

    Council or manager responds

    Council or management can assign, comment on and update the ticket.

Services marketplace

Local services, right inside STRATINK™

Residents can book local service providers directly through STRATINK™, and property managers can source and manage preferred vendors.

Cleaning

Cleaning services and housekeeping for regular or one-time resident support, plus carpet and window cleaning for move-in, move-out and seasonal needs.

Everyday help & trades

Dog walking, handyman services, small repairs, electricians, plumbers and appliance repair from local providers.

Move support

Movers, junk removal and move-out cleaning, with elevator reservations and move approvals visible to management.

Package tracking

Delivery notices, pickup confirmation and package history inside the resident app.

Building services for managers

Community marketplace

Residents can buy, sell, give away and post lost-and-found items within their own building, with admin moderation.

STRATINK™ is designed to connect residents with local providers without the building hiring service crews.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the pilot cost?

Founding Buildings get preferred early pricing. Contact us at hello@stratink.ca for details.

How is our data handled?

Your information is handled under our Privacy Policy. STRATINK™ is designed with role-based access, so documents, requests and resident profiles are organised by access level. We plan a privacy and security review before launch.

How long does setup take?

We set your building up with you, including resident invitations.

Who can use STRATINK™?

Residents, owners, tenants, council members, property managers and admins, each with a view suited to their role.

Do we need to switch property managers?

No. STRATINK™ is designed to work alongside your existing property manager.

Which devices does it work on?

Use STRATINK™ on your phone or computer.

What can we do with documents?

Councils and managers upload and organise bylaws, AGM minutes, move-in rules, forms and building records; residents find them in the app instead of searching email.

How does the services marketplace work?

The services marketplace is part of STRATINK™. Residents can book local service providers, and managers can source and manage preferred vendors, inside the app.

About

Our story

We're builders by trade. Our founders work in construction, so we understand how buildings are built, maintained and repaired, and what it takes to keep them running well.

We also live in strata buildings ourselves. That means we see the everyday problems first-hand: notices scattered across lobby boards and inboxes, maintenance requests that move slowly, documents that are hard to find when you need them, and email chains that never seem to end.

We knew strata living could work better. So we built STRATINK™ to bring it all together: one clear place where councils, residents and property managers can share notices, track maintenance and find the documents that matter.

STRATINK™ is built in British Columbia, for BC strata, by people who know these buildings from the ground floor up and call them home.
